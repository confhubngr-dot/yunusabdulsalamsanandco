About Us

More than legal advisors — strategic partners

A distinguished law firm with over 15 years of excellence — best known for debt recovery, property law, litigation, and corporate practice.

We serve clients across real estate, banking and finance, corporate and commercial law, and secured credit transactions, delivering results-driven services that meet international best practices while upholding the highest ethics at the Bar.

The firm is led by Yunus Abdulsalam, SAN, a respected Senior Advocate of Nigeria. We are more than legal advisors — we are strategic partners who help clients navigate complex challenges with confidence.
